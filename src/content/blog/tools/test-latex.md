## 3. 微積分與極限 (Calculus)
$$
\int_{a}^{b} f(x) \,dx = F(b) - F(a)
$$

$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} \left( 1 + \frac{1}{n} \right)^n = e
$$

## 4. 矩陣 (Matrices)
$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 1 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
\times
\begin{pmatrix}
x \\
y \\
z
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
x \\
y \\
z
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## 5. 條件分支 (Cases)
$$
f(n) =
\begin{cases} 
n/2 & \text{if } n \text{ is even} \\
3n+1 & \text{if } n \text{ is odd}
\end{cases}
$$

## 6. 複雜求和 (Summation)
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i^3 = \left( \frac{n(n+1)}{2} \right)^2
$$
