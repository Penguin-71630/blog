## Instruction Format


<InfoBox style="info">

R-Type Instruction

$$
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
op & rs & rt & rd & shamt & funct \\
\hline
000000 & 00000 & 00000 & 00000 & 00000 & 000000 \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

</InfoBox>

<InfoBox style="success">

I-Type Instruction

$$
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
op & rs & rt & rd & shamt & funct \\
\hline
000000 & 00000 & 00000 & 00000 & 00000 & 000000 \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

Example:
- `addi $t0, $t1, 5` → `001000 01001 01000 0000000000000101`

</InfoBox>

<InfoBox style="warning">

J-Type Instruction

$$
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
op & rs & rt & rd & shamt & funct \\
\hline
000000 & 00000 & 00000 & 00000 & 00000 & 000000 \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

</InfoBox>
